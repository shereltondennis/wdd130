Monrovia City

Monrovia is the capital and largest city of Liberia, West Africa, founded by freed American slaves in 1822 and named for U.S. President James Monroe; it's the nation's political, economic, and cultural hub, located on the Atlantic coast with a significant port, but still rebuilding after devastating civil wars. The city features a diverse population, historical landmarks like the National Museum, and a tropical climate with abundant rainfall, serving as Liberia's center for education and commerce. It was established by the American Colonization Society for freed slaves, named Christopolis in 1822, then renamed Monrovia in 1824 after President James Monroe.
